# Source

Choose **what** to render and **how long**.

- **Range** — the time span: *Custom* (a start/end you set), *Loop* (the timeline's loop range),
  *Soundtrack* (the main soundtrack's length), or *Continuous* (keep recording until you press stop — great
  for live sets).
- **Scale** — whether Start/End are in musical *bars*, *seconds*, or *frames*.
- **FPS** — frames per second of the output (60 is a good default).
- **Resolution Scale** — a multiplier on the output size. Set it to 2 for 4K from a 1080p comp.
- **Motion Blur** — only for projects using [RenderWithMotionBlur]; overrides its quality just for the export.

Hover the **(?)** next to any field for a short explanation. Click below for the full guide.
