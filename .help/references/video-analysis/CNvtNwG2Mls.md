A four-and-a-half-hour community session that experiments with blending text-based SDFs, walks through the new player-export and shader-caching improvements, builds a text-outline particle emitter with lifetime attribute mapping, dissects an anaglyph stereo camera setup (including live-coding a camera-reference input for [CamPosition]), creates [RemapVec3] on the spot, and presents the upcoming physical-space output-management board.
