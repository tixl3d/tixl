A community meetup where the maintainer previews the new output-management/projection-mapping system for 4.3 (content→surface→output abstraction, calibration, setups), answers point-color questions with deep [CustomPointShader] live-coding, extends [BlendPoints] on stream, and prototypes a drone-show pipeline with particle forces.
