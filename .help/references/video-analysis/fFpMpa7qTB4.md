A nearly four-hour community meetup centered on TiXL's custom shader family: fixing the raymarch perspective-shift and [SortPoints] bugs, an extended live exploration of the new custom draw mesh operator (fields, point attributes, lights, image inputs), plus the 4.2 release, the backup/recovery system, and a roadmap discussion covering glTF character animation, reroute nodes, and a future shader graph.
