A long meet-up built around the Linux port: how DirectX/SharpDX and WinForms were swapped for a thin Vulkan facade and SDL, why existing HLSL shaders survived the move, and a live demo of the editor running natively on Arch. The second half is dense community Q&A — how time, bars and external MIDI/Ableton clocks actually work, why an inverse-switch "route node" doesn't fit the pull-based evaluation model, reading the performance numbers, render-target and resolution economics, a full photo-based projector calibration walked through with a user, and a closing discovery of screen-space derivatives for anti-aliasing field edges in a custom shader.
