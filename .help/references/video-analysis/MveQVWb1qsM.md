A long meet-up. It opens with a first look at the CPU geometry-node system coming in 4.3 (Voronoi fracture, bevel, C# deferred scalar functions, chunk drawing) and the output-management board being rebuilt (reference-image straightening, surfaces, slices, projector calibration). Then come demo walkthroughs: a feedback/attractor Evoke entry, and a long tour of the Supercluster demo project covering timeline clips, dope-sheet auto-pin, a letterbox via SliceViewPort, camera shake, player export and a sprite-text bug hunt. Community members show laser output, AI nodes and mesh-to-SDF, and it closes on how to get pitch and velocity out of loaded MIDI clips.
