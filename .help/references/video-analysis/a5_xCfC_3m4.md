A long live meet-up covering the new asset-library/path overhaul, a performance-monitor walkthrough and GPU benchmarking, an end-to-end MediaPipe hand-tracking particle build, authoring SkillQuest tutorials, and community demos (Assimp import, AI shader generation, VAT instancing).

## Mentions
- 19:51→23:25 [ui:PerformanceMonitor] · in-depth · explained · Concept · 88% — Reading the top-left graph stack: top line is frame *duration* in ms (16 ms = 60 fps budget), then UI-update time, then per-frame allocations (~80 fine, ~500 risks drops), plus draw-calls and shader-dispatch counts to spot the real bottleneck.
- 23:25→29:40 [FractalNoise] · explained · experiment · Performance · 82% — Drive it from [Time] and crank the output resolution to stress fill-rate: a procedural noise this heavy stays real-time at 256² but falls below 60 fps at 4K, making it a quick way to gauge a GPU's shader throughput.
- 26:00→26:40 [ui:EvaluationContext] · explained · discussion · Concept · 78% — Why a static node renders dimmed/cached and only goes opaque while recomputing: an output that never changes is cached and costs no shader load until a parameter forces re-evaluation.
- 30:50→33:20 [Text] [Loop] · explained · experiment · Performance · 80% — To benchmark CPU-bound draw-call cost rather than fill-rate, wrap one [Text] in a [Loop] and feed the loop's [GetFloatVar] progress into a position offset — each iteration is a separate draw call, and frame time climbs past ~200 calls regardless of GPU.
- 34:00→58:00 [ui:AssetLibrary] · in-depth · explained · Concept · 90% — The new browser lists every asset across all open projects, filters by type and by which operators can consume each, shows usage dots that jump to the referencing operator, and supports drag-out and external-file drop (copied into the project's asset folder).

- 1:24:50→1:26:50 [ConvertFormat] · explained · experiment · Gotcha · 80% — Camera frames arrive as BGRA8 while TiXL works in 16-bit RGB; if a downstream detector misbehaves on a flipped or recolored image, convert the texture to the format the consumer expects rather than fighting the channel order.

- 1:29:55→1:31:00 [FilterPoints] · explained · experiment · Example · 80% — Isolate specific landmarks (e.g. index 4 and 8) by index, then combine two filtered results, to pull just the thumb-and-fingertip pair out of a larger point buffer.
- 1:31:30→1:33:00 [BlendPoints] · explained · experiment · Example · 80% — Blending two single-point buffers yields their midpoint on the GPU — a cheap way to derive a center anchor (here between two fingertips) without any CPU readback.
- 1:33:00→1:34:30 [PointSimulation] · in-depth · experiment · Gotcha · 85% — Useful as a damping/smoothing pass on a noisy point buffer, but it behaves like a particle sim and reassigns point indices — so it breaks any setup that depends on stable per-point identity. - 1:43:00→1:45:00 [Remap] · explained · experiment · Example · 78% — Rescale a raw measured value (here a finger-gap distance) into a usable parameter range, then flip/curve the range so "fingers close" maps to the velocity you actually want.
