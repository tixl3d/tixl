A short tip tour of the [Rings] operator, walking every parameter that shapes its concentric-ring pattern and pointing out which ones reward animation.

## Mentions
- 0:09→0:16 [Rings] · passing · scripted · Tip · 80% — Alt-hovering its presets is the fastest way to preview the huge range of looks one operator can produce before touching any knob.
- 0:20→0:47 [Rings] · explained · scripted · Parameters · 88% — Inner and outer radius set the band's extent, count multiplies the rings, and a rotate plus offset pair drives radial spin and trippy outward travel for animated loops.
- 0:47→1:14 [Rings] · explained · scripted · Parameters · 88% — A segment ratio breaks each ring into arcs, and most knobs expose a paired second value that randomizes that property per ring (segments, twist, thickness) so the pattern reads organic rather than uniform.
- 1:16→1:36 [Rings] · explained · scripted · Parameters · 85% — Fill ratio thins the rings toward a logo-like sparse look, the highlight color/seed picks out a random subset of rings, and feeding the seed an oscillator animates which rings light up.
- 1:36→1:49 [Rings] · explained · scripted · Parameters · 85% — A distort knob bends the flat rings into a perspective tunnel, while contrast reshapes the fill falloff toward a soft gradient ramp.
