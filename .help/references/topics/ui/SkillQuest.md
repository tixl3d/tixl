A guided, hands-on learning track that teaches TiXL by having you rebuild small scenes step by step.

Open it from the **home panel** (it can be toggled on or off in the settings). Levels are laid out as a [ui:SkillMap|Skill Map] of connected topics — basics, then images and gradients, points, particles and fields — and each one unlocks once you finish its prerequisites. In a level you're shown a hidden reference output and must wire up the graph to match it; later levels also compare the animated value over time, so you have to reason about the curve, not just the still image.
