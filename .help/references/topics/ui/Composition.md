A **Composition** is the operator you currently have open in the [ui:Graph|graph] — the sub-graph whose contents you're editing and viewing.

As you dive into an operator, it becomes the active composition and its output drives the [ui:OutputWindow|Output window]. Everything you place, wire and animate happens inside the current composition, and stepping back out returns you to its parent. Think of it as "where you are" in the nested structure of a project.
