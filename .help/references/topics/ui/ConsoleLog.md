The window that lists TiXL's log messages — status notes, warnings, and errors — so you can see what the editor and your operators are doing.

Messages arrive with timestamps and color-coded severity; a filter field at the top narrows the list to matching text, and a clear button empties it. Many lines are clickable and jump straight to the operator that produced them, which makes it the first place to look when an operator reports a warning or something isn't rendering. Watch it after loading a project or wiring up a new operator chain to catch problems early.
