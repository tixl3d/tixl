**Annotations** are labeled, colored boxes you draw behind operators in the [ui:Graph|graph] to group and comment on parts of your project.

They have no effect on rendering — they're purely there to organize a busy graph into readable sections and remind you (or a collaborator) what each cluster does. Add one from the graph, then resize it to frame the operators it belongs to and give it a title. Combined with tidy operator alignment, annotations keep a complex composition legible as it grows.
