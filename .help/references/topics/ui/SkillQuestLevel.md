A single lesson inside a [ui:SkillQuest|Skill Quest]: an editable graph you complete to reproduce a hidden reference output.

Each level gives you the reference result, a partly-built solution that feeds it, and a few loose building blocks to wire in. On-screen **tour points** walk you through the steps with short instructions (for example, "connect this to the point's position") and blink to highlight the operator or parameter you should touch. Follow the steps until your output matches the reference, then move on to the levels it unlocks.
