The map of [ui:SkillQuest|Skill Quest] levels laid out as connected cells, showing which lessons you've unlocked and what each one leads to.

You reach it from the Skill Quest on the **home panel**; completing a level opens the ones that depend on it, so the map doubles as your progress overview. Under the hood it's also an authoring tool: level makers use it to lay out topics as cells and set the unlock rules — whether a cell needs one prerequisite finished or all of them.
