The viewer that shows the rendered result of the selected operator — the image or 3D scene you are building.

Whatever operator you pin or select renders here in real time as you adjust parameters. The toolbar fits or zooms the view (1:1 and Fit), toggles gizmos and camera controls, sets the preview resolution, and saves a full-resolution screenshot of the current frame to the project's screenshot folder. In fill mode the view matches the panel's height and pads the sides to your set aspect ratio, so resizing the panel reflows the image without distorting it.

The view menu (the operator-name dropdown left of the 1:1 button) also has a Texture Filtering option: Linear keeps the image smooth at every zoom level, Point shows the rendered pixels unfiltered so individual texels read as crisp blocks, and Auto uses Linear while the image is shown smaller than its resolution and switches to Point at its native size or larger.
