The **Timeline** is where you animate parameters over time, laying down keyframes and playing them back in sync with music or an audio track.

Open it along the bottom of the editor; time runs in musical bars by default, so animation stays locked to the beat. It holds the [ui:DopeSheet|Dope sheet] for arranging keyframes and an expandable [ui:AnimationArea|Animation area] with an inline [ui:CurveEditor|curve editor] for shaping their values and tangents. You can also drag an mp3 straight onto it to add an audio clip, and overlap two clips in time to crossfade between them.
