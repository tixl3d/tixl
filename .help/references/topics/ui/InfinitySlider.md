The **Infinity slider** is the drag overlay that lets you push a numeric parameter far beyond its normal slider range, with no fixed end stop.

It appears as a wheel-like dial when you drag a value: moving horizontally turns the dial to change the value, and moving vertically changes how fine each turn is, so you can jump across large ranges and still land on precise numbers. Parameters clamped on a min or max still respect those limits, but otherwise you can dial a value as high or low as you need.
