The **Control bar** is the [ui:Timeline]'s transport row, where you play, pause, loop, and scrub through time and set the playback speed.

Use it to jump to the start, step to the previous or next keyframe, and play forward or backward; pressing play repeatedly doubles the speed for fast scrubbing. It also shows the current time in bars, so you can line up your edits with the beat while the animation runs.
