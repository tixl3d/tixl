The startup dialog shown when you launch TiXL, introducing the current version and pointing you toward getting started.

It appears automatically on first launch and can be reopened from the **Help** menu. From here you can read what's new in the release notes, find tutorials and community links on the **Getting Started** page, and jump back into one of your projects from the **Projects** page; a "show on startup" toggle controls whether it greets you each time. For preview builds, see the separate [ui:WelcomeAlphaWindow|Welcome alpha window], which also helps migrate settings and projects and links the guided feature tests.
