The home view that lists your projects and lets you open, create, or manage them.

Each entry shows the project's name, namespace, folder, and thumbnail; click one to load it, or use the **Add Project** button to start a new one. Right-click a project to reveal it in Explorer, unload it, or archive it, and reactivate archived projects from the separate list below. Project data lives under your Documents folder (relocatable in the [ui:Settings]), while settings, logs, and backups sit under AppData.
