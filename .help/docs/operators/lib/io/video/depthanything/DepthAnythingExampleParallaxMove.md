# DepthAnythingExampleParallaxMove

*in [Lib.io.video.depthanything](README.md)*

Shifts a still image by its depth, as if the camera moved sideways. Animate Offset for the classic 2.5D wobble; FocalPlane chooses the depth that stays still. MinDepth and MaxDepth come from DepthAnything.
